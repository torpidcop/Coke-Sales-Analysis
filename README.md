# 🥤 Coca-Cola Sales Analysis (Excel)

An Excel-based analysis of Coca-Cola sales across key American retailers, built to turn transactional and operational data into actionable business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Coca-Cola sales data using **Microsoft Excel** to evaluate performance across retailers, regions, and time periods. It focuses on core metrics such as **total sales**, **units sold**, and **operating margin** to identify areas of strength and opportunities for improvement.

## 🎯 Objectives

The analysis aims to understand:

- How Coca-Cola sales vary by **retailer**, **region**, and **time**
- The relationship between **pricing**, **volume**, and **profitability**
- Trends in **operating margins** across different markets

## 📊 Key Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Revenue generated from product sales |
| Units Sold | Volume of products sold |
| Price per Unit | Average selling price |
| Operating Profit | Profit after operating costs |
| Operating Margin | Operating profit as a percentage of sales |

## 🔍 Analysis Areas

1. **Regional Performance**: Comparing sales and margins across U.S. regions to find top and underperforming markets.
2. **Retailer Efficiency**: Evaluating which retailers drive the most revenue and which deliver the healthiest margins.
3. **Sales Trends Over Time**: Tracking monthly and seasonal patterns in sales and units sold.
4. **Pricing, Volume & Profitability**: Examining how price points and sales volume influence operating profit and margin.
5. **Operating Margin Trends**: Identifying how margins shift across markets and periods.
